# Hearth

This is a functional heat source. Place the vessel on the chimney top, not beside the fire. Open the hearth and supply furnace fuel. An extinguished or waterlogged heat source supplies no heat.

Use it to heat a [Tea Kettle](herbalwitch:stations/kettle.md); the [Witch's Stove](herbalwitch:stations/stove.md) page shows an alternative arrangement.
