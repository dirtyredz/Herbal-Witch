# Restful Infusion

Brew [lavender powder](herbalwitch:apothecary/lavender/powder.md) and [valerian powder](herbalwitch:apothecary/valerian/powder.md) in a heated [Tea Kettle](herbalwitch:stations/kettle.md). Fill a glass bottle by using it on water, then follow the live recipe for this server's inputs and returns.

<HerbalRecipe id="herbalwitch:processing/steeping/restful_infusion" />

Hold the finished drink and use it until you finish drinking. It resets your time since rest, helping with phantoms without forcing sleep, and returns an empty bottle. You can use it before becoming a witch. A witch can also use it to prepare for their next rewarded sleep.
