# Tea Kettle

Place the kettle on a lit [Witch's Stove](herbalwitch:stations/stove.md) or on the chimney top of a functional [hearth](herbalwitch:stations/hearth.md). Open the kettle and place powders in separate ingredient slots and the water bottle in the base-liquid slot.
