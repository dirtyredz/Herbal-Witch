# Fresh valerian

Harvest a mature valerian plant by using it. Grow more with [valerian seeds](herbalwitch:apothecary/valerian/seeds.md).

Put the fresh herb into a [Herb Drying Rack](herbalwitch:stations/drying_rack.md). Drying starts automatically without fuel; keep the area loaded and leave room for the output.
