# Art - The Herbal Witch

Direction, palette, materials, pixel density, provenance, and the owner review checklist.

## Owner direction (2026-10-21)

Quote: "All of the colour options are terrible." Decision: the 18 finishes are gone. The mod has one
design, "cottage": dark wood, deep green and hot pink accents, a little dark purple, vine leaves hanging
off furniture, plain fabric, no flower patterns. Stations, the oil lantern and the hung bundles were
authored in this direction from the start. This replaces the Milestone 1 art questions.

## Palette

| Role | Colours |
|---|---|
| Dark wood | #3B2416, #5A3A22 |
| Deep green | #2F5A2C, #36643A |
| Hot pink | #D8407A, #C93A70 |
| Dark purple (small amounts) | #3E1F47, #4A2656 |

Brass, glass and tincture colours keep their own tones.

## Rules

- **Materials** (`textures/block/material/`, 32 x 32, generated by `tools/generate-textures.mjs`), all
  untinted: `dark_wood`, `vine`, `brass`, `glass` (cutout, alpha 0/255), `tincture` (three colour bands
  picked by UV), `fabric_green`, `fabric_pink`, `trim_purple`, `rug`, `lamp_shade`. There is no tint
  index and no colour handler anywhere.
- **Herb and item sprites** are 16 px with binary alpha (0 or 255). Each herb has four distinct growth
  silhouettes (`stage_0..3`), so ages read at a glance. Seed, fresh-herb, bundle, powder and product
  sprites are generated in `tools/art/botany/` and `tools/art/preparations/`.
- **Stations, lantern, bundles:** authored cuboid templates (`models/block/stations/`,
  `models/block/preparations/templates/`) with materials from `tools/art/stations/materials.mjs` and
  `tools/art/preparations/materials.mjs`. The herb bed frame is dark wood.
- **GUI:** the five station interface atlases (`textures/gui/stations/`) are generated; the layout
  (slot positions, arrows, status area) is in `tools/art/stations/interfaces.mjs`.
- **Models:** Blockbench-style cuboid JSON in `models/block/decor/templates/`, explicit UVs, within one
  block, axis-aligned. Templates bind their textures directly and blockstates use the templates
  directly. One UV unit per model pixel (two texels per pixel) on every face except glass, which
  stretches its rim over each face. Keep ordinary furniture under about 30 elements.
- **Render types:** solid by default; `minecraft:cutout` only where a template has glass or leaves.
- **Provenance:** every texture is generated from seeded code in `tools/art/` or is authored JSON for
  this mod. Nothing is copied from vanilla or other mods. MIT.

## Owner review checklist (0.1.0-dev.4)

Status: **awaiting owner review.** In the trial instance, creative mode. The quickest way to see
everything: on a flat plot run `/herbalwitch showcase` (operator), then walk it.

1. **Single furniture design.** Open "Cottage Decor". Do the five pieces read as one set in dark wood?
   Check the green and pink accents, the vine leaves, the plain fabric, the brass, the lamp shade lit
   and unlit. Turn armchairs and counters in all four directions; hang a shelf; stand the lamp on a
   floor and a counter.
2. **Herbs.** Open "Gardening". Each of the 14 herbs: do the four growth stages read as different,
   and do seed and fresh-herb icons tell herbs apart at hotbar size? Check the herb bed dry and moist.
3. **Stations.** Place the drying rack, mortar, kettle, hearth and infusion pot (the kettle and pot on a
   fuelled hearth). Check models, the hanging herbs on a stocked rack, the lit hearth, each screen
   layout and its status line.
4. **Products.** Open "Apothecary": bundles on a wall and ceiling, powders, drinks, the salve, oils,
   garden products, the oil lantern lit and unlit.
5. **Context.** Items in hand, on the ground and in item frames; shaders on and off; a resource reload
   (F3+T).

Feedback wanted: furniture "keep / change (what)"; per herb "stages readable / change"; per station
"model and UI OK / change (what)"; palette adjustments; anything missing from the look.
