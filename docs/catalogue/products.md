# Product catalogue

Traces every product category in the design (everyday, combat, familiar preparations) and the 14 named herbs to station, delivery, witch requirement, effect and milestone. Effects and recipes are specified when the owning milestone starts; "Planned effect" is the design intent plus plan section 7 duration defaults.

Duration defaults: utility 60-120 s, combat control 5-10 s, familiar buffs 180 s. Same effects refresh, not stack. Sleep wakes on damage; bosses and protected targets are immune to hard control by default. Harmful player targeting is off by default (PvP). Stations: see stations.md. Non-witches can buy and use finished products; "Witch-only?" refers to making them.

## Herbs

Milestone 2. Starter four first (lavender, sage, mint, chamomile), then the remaining ten. Traits (design set: restorative, calming, toxic, protective, growth, attraction, binding, sight, speed) are fictional game properties fixed when milestone 2 starts, except where the plan names them. Every progression-critical herb stays cultivable once acquired; weather, night and moon only improve discovery or yield.
