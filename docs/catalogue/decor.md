# Decor catalogue

Traces every furniture piece on the cottage concept sheet (13 room/category groups) to a planned piece id, family, function, milestone and status. Block/item IDs are `herbalwitch:decor/<family>/<piece>/<palette>/<finish>`; families: seating, storage, surfaces, lighting, textiles, fixtures, openings.

Conventions: an alias row reuses the canonical piece id and inherits its milestone and status. Function terms follow plan section 7 (chairs/benches seat, beds sleep, cabinets store, display shelves hold items, doors operate, lights illuminate; plumbing, mirrors, fridge and TV stand are decorative unless assigned). Milestones: 5A kitchen/dining/apothecary/storage, 5B living/bedroom/office/entryway, 5C bath/garden/lighting/shelves/textiles/openings.

## Finishes

18 cosmetic finishes in two palettes; each is one frame material plus one coordinated fabric colour, not an 18x18 cross-product. They add no tree species and change no strength or recipe cost. Acquisition: shaped crafting gives dark_oak; a stonecutter converts any finish of a piece into any other finish of the same piece, one-for-one.

## Counting

The sheet is not an exact manifest of ~120 unique models. Repeated illustrations (Wall Shelf, Console Table, Bench, Mirror, Small Table, Archway, Rug) map to one piece id unless the form differs (plan section 6). Each piece is generated in all 18 finishes. Milestone 1 implements five pieces x 18 finishes = 90 blocks.
