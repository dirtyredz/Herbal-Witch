# Decor catalogue

Traces every furniture piece on the cottage concept sheet (13 room/category groups) to a planned piece id, family, function, milestone and status. Block/item IDs are `herbalwitch:decor/<family>/<piece>` (one cottage design, no finish segment); families: seating, storage, surfaces, lighting, textiles, fixtures, openings.

Conventions: an alias row reuses the canonical piece id and inherits its milestone and status. Function terms follow plan section 7 (chairs/benches seat, beds sleep, cabinets store, display shelves hold items, doors operate, lights illuminate; plumbing, mirrors, fridge and TV stand are decorative unless assigned). Milestones: 5A kitchen/dining/apothecary/storage, 5B living/bedroom/office/entryway, 5C bath/garden/lighting/shelves/textiles/openings.

## Design

There is one cottage design (2026-10-21), restyled in 0.2.0-dev.1 (the art pass; owner review
pending; docs/ART.md): dark brown and warm wood, forest green, magenta and plum, gold bands, rivets
and studs, ivy with small pink flowers. It replaces the former 18 finishes; `FinishCatalog`
keeps a single entry so a design can return later. The pieces are crafted directly from dark oak inputs:

| Piece | Id | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| Armchair | `decor/seating/armchair` | `PWP` / `PPP` / `S S`: dark oak planks, any wool, sticks |
| Apothecary Counter | `decor/surfaces/apothecary_counter` | `SSS` / `PNP` / `PPP`: dark oak slabs, planks, a gold nugget |
| Small Wall Shelf (2) | `decor/storage/small_wall_shelf` | `SSS` / `T T`: dark oak slabs, sticks |
| Table Lamp | `decor/lighting/table_lamp` | wool over a torch over a dark oak slab |
| Small Rug (2) | `decor/textiles/small_rug` | `C S C`: two carpets (any colour) and string |

### Look (0.2.0-dev.1)

Same five ids; no new furniture ids in this release.

- **Armchair:** dark brown frame with chunky capped posts and magenta inlays, gold and plum bands;
  forest-green velvet seat and tall back with magenta tufting studs and piping; draped arm and head
  runners with pointed hems; dense trailing ivy with small pink flowers.
- **Apothecary Counter:** apothecary chest in warm wood with black iron corner brackets and gold
  rivets; two deep-green drawer fronts in gold frames with chunky gold knobs; gold studs along the top
  rail; a magenta runner draped over the top and down the front; a dried bundle on a gold ring; ivy.
- **Small Wall Shelf:** the approved bottle composition kept; iron brackets with gold rivets, a gold
  edge, richer ivy.
- **Table Lamp:** faceted magenta-and-plum shade with gold bands on a dark stem with gold and plum
  bands; the lit shade glows.
- **Small Rug:** dark green woven field, magenta and plum woven border with a gold line, a gold moon
  with stars and small pink flowers at its heart, gold tassels at each end.

## Counting

The sheet is not an exact manifest of ~120 unique models. Repeated illustrations (Wall Shelf, Console Table, Bench, Mirror, Small Table, Archway, Rug) map to one piece id unless the form differs (plan section 6). Milestone 1 implements five pieces, one block each.
